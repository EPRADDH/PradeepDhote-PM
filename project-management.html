Case Study 1

Multi-Region Private 5G Deployment for Smart Manufacturing ($25M Budget)

Timeline 18 Months
Capital Budget $25,000,000
Team Size 45 Engineers (3 Squads)
Methodology Hybrid PMP / SAFe

Business Objective

Deploy a high-availability Private 5G Standalone network across 6 automated manufacturing plants to replace unreliable Wi-Fi and support autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) with ultra-low latency requirements (<5ms).

Stakeholders & Governance

Executive Steering Committee (VP Manufacturing, CTO, VP Operations), Plant General Managers, RF Field Engineers, Fiber Contractors, and Vendor Account Leads.

Risk Management & Mitigation Matrix

  • • Risk: CBRS Radio Frequency interference from adjacent radar bands.
    Mitigation: Implemented SAS dynamic spectrum allocation with automated channel hopping.
  • • Risk: Global supply chain delay for gNodeB radio hardware.
    Mitigation: Dual-sourced radio hardware vendors and phased plant cutovers.

Business Results & Lessons Learned:

Delivered 3 weeks ahead of schedule and $1.2M under budget. Eliminated Wi-Fi dead zones, increasing factory floor operational efficiency by 28%. Key lesson: Early involvement of local OT plant technicians is essential for seamless wireless acceptance testing.

Case Study 2

Nationwide Autonomous AI-EMS Migration ($40M Scope)

Timeline 12 Months
Scope $40,000,000
Stakeholders 120+ Engineers & NOC Ops
Methodology Agile Scrum

Objective & Execution: Migrated legacy SNMP-based element management system monitoring 100,000+ national cell towers to a modern, AI-driven Kafka/Kubernetes telemetry platform capable of self-healing anomaly detection.

Results: Reduced alarm noise by 45%, decreased ticket MTTR by 68%, and saved $6.2M in annual maintenance overhead.
